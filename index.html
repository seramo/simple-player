    Frequently asked questions

    Is Simple Player free?

    Yes. It is free, with no account and nothing to install.

    Are my videos uploaded?

    No. Your browser opens the file and plays it on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

    How do I play an MKV file online?

    Click Open video and pick the MKV file, or drop it on the page. It plays right away, including MKV files with HEVC, AC3 or DTS.

    Can I play a video from a URL?

    Yes. Paste a direct link to an MP4, WebM or M3U8 (HLS) video and press Play.

    Why won't my video play?

    Most files play, including HEVC, AC3 and DTS. If a link doesn't play, the site may block it, need a login, or it may not be a direct video link.

    Can I play MP3 and other audio files?

    Yes. Open or drop MP3, FLAC, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG or Opus files and they play like a video. Open a music folder to get a playlist with shuffle and repeat.

    How do I add subtitles to a video?

    Drop an SRT or VTT file on the page, or pick Add subtitle file in the subtitles menu. Subtitles with the same name as the video load automatically.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. It works in modern browsers on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone.
